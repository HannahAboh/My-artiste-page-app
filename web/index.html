Questions

Good to know

Do I need to know how to build websites?

Not at all. Paste your Spotify link and your page fills itself in. After that you change words, photos and colours from your dashboard, like editing a profile.

I make podcasts, not music. Can I use it?

Yes. Paste your Spotify podcast link instead and your page is set up for episodes.

What happens if a payment fails?

We email you and your page stays live for 7 days while you sort it out. If it's still unpaid after that, fans see a "coming back soon" page until you pay. Nothing is ever deleted.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time from your account. Your page stays up until the end of the time you've paid for.

How do I pay?

By debit or credit card on Paystack's secure checkout. We never see or store your card number.
